# Unit 3: Working in an office

## Vocabulary

| Word | Meaning | Translation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| deadline | the time by which a task must be finished | date limite |
| colleague | a person you work with | collègue |
| schedule | a plan of when things will happen | emploi du temps |
| promotion | a move to a more important job | promotion |

## Reading: A busy Monday

Sarah arrives at the office at eight o'clock every morning. Her manager usually sets up a meeting with the whole team on Mondays.
Today the team must finish the report before the deadline. Sarah checks her schedule and answers her emails quickly.
Her colleague Tom often helps her when the work becomes stressful. They follow up on every request from their clients.
After lunch, the manager announces that Sarah will get a promotion next month. Everyone congratulates her.
Good communication helps a team to meet its deadlines. A clear schedule reduces stress and improves productivity.

## Phrasal verbs

- set up – to arrange or organise something
- follow up – to find out more or take further action
- put off – to delay doing something
- fill in – to do someone's job for a short time

The meeting was put off until Friday because the manager was ill. Tom filled in for Sarah while she was on holiday.
