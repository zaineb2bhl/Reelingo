# Unit 8: Healthy habits

## Vocabulary

- habit – something you do often, almost without thinking
- exercise – physical activity to stay fit
- balanced diet – food that contains the right amounts of everything you need
- stress – a feeling of worry caused by a difficult situation

## Reading: Small changes, big results

Doctors say that adults should sleep seven to nine hours every night. Many people sleep less because they use their phones in bed.
Walking for thirty minutes a day is a simple form of exercise. A balanced diet includes fruit, vegetables, grains and some protein.
Drinking enough water helps your body and your brain. Stress can cause headaches and make it hard to sleep.
A good habit takes about two months to form, so it is important to be patient.

## Advice: should and shouldn't

We use should to give advice: You should drink more water.
We use shouldn't for things that are a bad idea: You shouldn't use your phone in bed.
