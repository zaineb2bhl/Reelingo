# Unit 6: Food and shopping

## Vocabulary

| Word | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| recipe | instructions for cooking a dish |
| ingredient | one of the foods used to make a dish |
| receipt | a piece of paper that shows what you paid |
| bargain | something you buy for less than its usual price |

## Reading: Cooking for friends

Maria wants to cook a vegetable soup for her friends on Saturday. She finds a simple recipe in an old cookbook.
The recipe needs carrots, onions, potatoes and a little salt. She writes a shopping list so she does not forget any ingredient.
At the market, the vegetables are fresh and cheap. A big bag of potatoes costs only two euros, which is a real bargain.
The cashier gives Maria a receipt, and she puts it in her purse. Her friends love the soup and ask for the recipe.

## Grammar: countable and uncountable nouns

We can count carrots and onions, so they are countable nouns. Salt, rice and water are uncountable nouns.
We say "a few carrots" but "a little salt". We use "much" with uncountable nouns in questions: How much salt do we need?
