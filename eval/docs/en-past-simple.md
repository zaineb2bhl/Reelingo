# Grammar: the past simple

## Form

We use the past simple for finished actions in the past. Regular verbs add -ed: work becomes worked and play becomes played.
Many common verbs are irregular. Go becomes went, see becomes saw and buy becomes bought.
In negative sentences we use did not and the base verb: She did not go to school yesterday.
In questions we use did and the base verb: Did you see the film last night?

## Time expressions

We often use yesterday, last week, two days ago and in 2010 with the past simple.

## Story: A weekend in London

Last weekend, Ahmed visited London with his sister. They took the train on Friday evening and arrived at ten o'clock.
On Saturday morning they walked along the river and saw Big Ben. In the afternoon they bought souvenirs at a market.
Ahmed did not like the weather because it rained all day on Sunday. They went home on Sunday night, tired but happy.
