# Unit 4: Travelling by train

## Vocabulary

- platform – the place where you get on a train
- ticket – a piece of paper that lets you travel
- delay – a time when something happens later than planned
- luggage – the bags you take on a journey

## Reading: A trip to the coast

We usually buy a ticket at the station before the journey. The train leaves from platform three at nine o'clock.
Passengers wait on the platform with their luggage. Yesterday there was a long delay because of the snow.
The conductor checked every ticket and helped an old man with his luggage. The journey to the coast takes two hours.
Trains are often faster than cars and more comfortable than buses.

## Useful phrases

- A return ticket, please.
- Which platform does the train leave from?
- Is this seat free?
